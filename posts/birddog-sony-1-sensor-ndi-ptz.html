BirdDog Release Sony 1" Sensor NDI PTZ Camera

Sony 1" Sensor. Size does matter.

The best images come from the best sensors, and the P4K is built exclusively around the best sensor in its category. The massive 1" Sony Exmor R CMOS backlit sensor delivers truly stunning pictures in all shoot modes and frame rates, whether in HD or 4K.

Full NDI. No compromises.

In the world of NDI® there are two flavours: NDI® and NDI|HX. NDI® is a variable bit rate, I-Frame codec that reaches around 140Mbits at 1080p60 and is visually lossless. NDI|HX is a compressed, long-GOP, H.264 variant that reaches around 12Mbits at 1080p60. The P4K is a full NDI® camera.
